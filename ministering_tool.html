Fetch/Print/Download

Tap Fetch/Print/Download to open that tab of LCR's Covenant Path Progress (sign in if asked). LCR has no export or download button for these pages, so you have to print the web page itself to a PDF from your browser: on a computer, use the browser's Print (Ctrl+P) and choose Save as PDF as the printer; on Android Chrome, ⋮ → Share → Print → Save as PDF. Then tap that list's Import and pick the file. You review everyone found before anything is added, and anyone on the list who isn't on the page any more is offered for removal. The PDF is read in your browser and isn't uploaded.

Households moving into the ward, from LCR's Members Moved In report. Importing the report adds anyone not already in Members or Households, geocodes new addresses, and puts each household on Move Ins with a status, Notes, and a running Next Step log you can tag people on. The Members Moved Out report further down removes people who have left, once you've confirmed each match.

Members Moved In Report

Fetch/Print/Download

Tap Fetch/Print/Download to open the Members Moved In report in LCR (sign in if asked) and print or save it as a PDF (on Android: Share → Print → Save as PDF), then tap Import and pick that file. The PDF is read in your browser and isn't uploaded; only the households you confirm are saved to the ward data.

Members Moved Out Report

Fetch/Print/Download

Tap Fetch/Print/Download to open the Members Moved Out report in LCR and save it as a PDF, then tap Import. Everyone in it is matched to Members (by name, checked against the age on file) and to their household; nothing is removed until you confirm.
